# The Avengers

## About My Favorite Movie

The Avengers is one of my favorite superhero movie franchises from Marvel Studios. It features a team of superheroes who work together to save the world.

### My Favorite Characters

- Iron Man – A genius inventor.
- Captain America – A brave and responsible leader.
- Thor – The God of Thunder.
- Hulk – A superhero with incredible strength.
- Black Widow – A skilled spy.
- Hawkeye – An expert archer.

### Why I Like The Avengers

I like this movie because of its exciting action scenes, amazing characters, and teamwork.
